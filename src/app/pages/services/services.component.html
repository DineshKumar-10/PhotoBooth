Premium photo booth experiences

Magic Mirror Photo Booth

An interactive, touchscreen mirror that allows guests to pose, see themselves, and take high-quality photos. Photos taken at the booth are printed instantly. To add fun and flavor, we provide goofy sunglasses, masks, cowboy hats, and colorful wigs. The digital booth also features welcome boards displaying the names and photos of the hosts or the couple. We customize prints with beautifully designed frames to match your specific event. It serves as an engaging entertainment hub during your celebration.

360-Degree Spin Selfie Booth

A popular, modern setup featuring a rotating camera platform that captures dynamic slow-motion and high-energy videos. Guests stand on a sturdy elevated circular platform, accommodating 1 to 3 people at a time. A motorized arm holds a smartphone with an LED ring light. Our operator starts the booth using a wireless remote. The camera rotates around guests while they dance or pose. Our software processes the video, adds music, slow-motion effects, and customized visual overlays. Guests can scan a QR code and download their videos directly to their smartphones.

Temporary Tattoos

An exciting extra activity station where guests can enjoy temporary tattoo and airbrush designs during the function. Our professionals use cosmetic inks designed for skin application. Each tattoo takes approximately 60 seconds to 3 minutes to apply. They dry quickly and typically last between 3 to 7 days, depending on the design and skin type. They can also be removed earlier using suitable cleaning methods.
