Magic Mirror Photo Booth: An interactive, touchscreen mirror that allows guests to pose, see themselves, and take high-quality photos and taken photos at the booth are printed instantly, to add fun and flavor to the photos, we provide a goofy sunglasses, masks, cowboy hats, and colorful wigs. 360-Degree Spin Selfie Booth

360-Degree Spin Selfie Booth: A popular, modern setup featuring a rotating camera platform that captures dynamic, slow-motion, or high-energy. A sturdy, elevated circular stage where guests stand. Usually accommodating anywhere from 1 to 3 people at a time. A motorized mechanical arm is attached to the base. It holds a smartphone with a bright LED ring light to ensure everyone looks flawless. Guests step onto the platform, and the operator starts the booth via a wireless remote. The arm spins rapidly around the platform 2 to 3 times while the guests dance or strike poses. Our software automatically processes the video in real-time speeding it up, slowing it down into slow-motion, adding music, and applying a customized visual border or overlay. Guests scan a QR code on a smart phone, and download the video directly to their phone via WhatsApp.

Temporary Tattoos

Temporary Tattoos: An extra activity station where guests can get temporary ink or airbrush designs applied during the function. Our Professionals use non-toxic, skin-friendly, and cosmetic inks, making it completely safe for both adults and children. Each tattoo takes anywhere from 60 seconds to 3 minutes to apply. They dry almost instantly and typically last between 3 to 7 days, though they can be easily scrubbed off earlier with soap, warm water, or baby oil.
